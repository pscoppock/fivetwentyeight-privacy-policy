Privacy Policy for 528

Last updated: October 12, 2026 · Applies to 528 on iPhone (version 2.0 and later, formerly "528 MCAT Math") and on the web at fivetwentysomething.com

528 is an adaptive MCAT® practice app covering Math, Chem/Phys equations and CARS question types. It works by keeping track of which questions you answer and how; that history is what lets it estimate what you already know and choose your next question. This policy explains exactly what is recorded, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Answer history: for each question you answer, the id of the question, the skill or equation it tests, whether your answer was correct, whether it was part of a knowledge check or practice, and the time.
  • How you answered: how long the question was on screen before you answered, whether you switched away from the app or browser tab while it was showing, and whether you chose "I don't know." These help tell a skill you have mastered from one you worked out slowly or looked up. They are never shown to anyone as a grade.
  • An anonymous id: a random identifier created for your installation of the app (or your browser). It is not derived from your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • Device type and app version: whether you use iPhone, Android, or the web version, and which version of 528.
  • An email address, only if you create an account (see Accounts below).

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or anything you type outside the app. An account is optional; without one, we have no email address for you.

Accounts (optional, web)

On the web you may create an account with an email address and password so you can pick up your progress on another device. Your email address and password are kept by Google Firebase Authentication only to sign you in and to send password-reset emails you ask for; we never see your password. Your answers stay filed under your anonymous id, apart from your email address, and your email address never appears in results, exports, or research.

If you create an account and finish a knowledge check during the web beta, your account may be given a founding-student number (the first 500), which is stored with your account so the founding offer can be honored when the beta ends.

Where it is stored

  • On your device (in the app, or in your browser's storage on the web), always.
  • In the cloud if cloud backup is on (it is on by default; you are asked when you first open the app and can change it at any time on the app's Privacy page). The backup is a copy of your answer history, keyed by your anonymous id, stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States. It lets your progress survive a reinstall, and with an account, follow you to another device.

How it is used

  • To run the adaptive knowledge checks and practice for you.
  • In aggregate, to improve the questions, the skill maps, and the timing and difficulty settings, and for research on how students learn MCAT content. Aggregate results never identify an individual, and your anonymous id and email address are never published.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app or on the website.

Price, subscriptions and payment

The web version is free during the beta. On iPhone, 528 Premium is a subscription sold through Apple's App Store (and, where available, Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the app never sees your payment details and only learns whether a subscription is active. If a paid option is added to the web version later, this policy will be updated before it starts.

Your choices

  • Turn cloud backup off on the app's Privacy page (shield icon). New answers then stay on your device only.
  • Delete your cloud data with the "Delete my cloud data" button on the same page. This removes everything stored under your anonymous id.
  • Reset progress within a section deletes those answers from your device and, if backup is on, from the cloud.
  • Delete your account by emailing us from the address on the account (see Contact). We will delete the account and its email address.
  • Request or delete data by email (see Contact). Include your anonymous id, shown on the Privacy page, so we can find your records — without it we cannot tell which records are yours. The "Contact support" button on the Privacy page fills it in for you.

Retention

Answer history is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above, or until you delete it. Account email addresses are kept until you delete the account. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear your browser's site data.

Accounts from earlier versions

Version 1 of this app (as "528 MCAT Math") let users create an account with an email address and username. Those records are retained only so that returning users can be recognised; they are not used for any other purpose, and you may ask for them to be deleted at any time using the contact below.

Children

528 is intended for students preparing for the MCAT and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that allow each installation or account to read and write only its own records.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will show the updated policy and the "last updated" date above will change.
