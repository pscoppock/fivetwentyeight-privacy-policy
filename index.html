Privacy Policy for 528

Last updated: September 21, 2026 · Applies to 528 version 2.0 and later (formerly "528 MCAT Math")

528 is an adaptive MCAT math practice app. It works by keeping track of which questions you answer and whether you got them right; that history is what lets it estimate what you already know and choose your next question. This policy explains exactly what is recorded, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Answer history: the id of each question you answer, the skill it tests, whether your answer was correct, whether it was part of a knowledge check or practice, and the time.
  • An anonymous id: a random identifier created for your installation of the app. It is not derived from your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • Device type: whether you use iPhone, Android, or the web version.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or anything you type outside the app. There is no account to create.

Where it is stored

  • On your device, always.
  • In the cloud if cloud backup is on (it is on by default; you are asked when you first open the app and can change it at any time on the app's Privacy page). The backup is a copy of your answer history, keyed by your anonymous id, stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in the United States. It lets your progress survive a reinstall.

How it is used

  • To run the adaptive knowledge check and practice for you.
  • In aggregate, to improve the question bank and the skill map, and for research on how students learn MCAT math. Aggregate results never identify an individual, and your anonymous id is never published.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. There are no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

Subscriptions and payment

528 Premium is a subscription sold through Apple's App Store (and, where available, Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the app never sees your payment details. The app only learns whether a subscription is active.

Your choices

  • Turn cloud backup off on the app's Privacy page (shield icon). New answers then stay on your device only.
  • Delete your cloud data with the "Delete my cloud data" button on the same page. This removes everything stored under your anonymous id.
  • Reset progress within a section deletes those answers from your device and, if backup is on, from the cloud.
  • Request or delete data by email (see Contact). Include your anonymous id, shown on the Privacy page, so we can find your records — without it we cannot tell which records are yours.

Retention

Answer history is kept for as long as it is useful for the purposes above, or until you delete it. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

Accounts from earlier versions

Version 1 of this app (as "528 MCAT Math") let users create an account with an email address and username. Those records are retained only so that returning users can be recognised; they are not used for any other purpose, and you may ask for them to be deleted at any time using the contact below.

Children

528 is intended for students preparing for the MCAT and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Security

Cloud data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules that allow each installation to read and write only its own records.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will show the updated policy and the "last updated" date above will change.
